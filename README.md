# Fair allocation of ressource in the moral dilemma of triage

The repository contains data and analysis files for the following journal article:

Keßler, Peer and Ivar Krumpal (2024): "Fair allocation of ressource in the moral dilemma of triage". *Forthcoming*.

## Abstract

Against the background of the COVID-19 pandemic that has shaken societies around the world, the debate about fairness of medical allocation decisions is gaining momentum. Studying a sample of a broad international public (\textit{N} = 1998), we investigate citizens' ethical preferences in the moral dilemma of triage decisions. Our objective is to provide empirical evidence of the link between welfare maximization on specific occasions (outcome fairness) and welfare maximization over time (procedural fairness), a link that has not yet been demonstrated. First, we address the key problem of which of several contradictory ethical criteria and principles of distributive justice should be used to determine the fairness of outcomes in triage situations. Preferences about outcome fairness are inferred from observed allocation decisions in a conjoint experiment. Second, preferences of procedural fairness are measured via fairness ratings of a series of triage procedures. Third, we analyze the relationship between the observed allocation outcomes and the fairness ratings of procedures. Our results support the hypothesized match between principles of outcome fairness and rules of procedural fairness. Finally, we review the current expert discourse and reflect it with the citizens ethical preferences observed in our study. We find that although citizens' preferences match expert ethics in prioritizing outcomes and procedures consistent with utilitarian principles, the overall picture is nuanced.

## Usage

The repository contains data (`data/data.R`) and the analysis file `kessler_krumpal_analysis.Rmd` as an R Notebook to replicate the results of the article.

Please do not hesitate to contact the authors for further information or remarks on our analysis.
